Getting started

One tap. After that, you only talk.

Most apps lose blind and older people at the sign-up form. Vosa has no form. It asks, you answer.

  1. Tap anywhere on the screen

    The whole screen is the button, so there is nothing to find.

  2. Answer out loud

    Your language, your name, your phone number. Vosa repeats each one and waits for your yes.

  3. Choose a secret phrase

    Two or three words only you know. Vosa never shows them and never says them back.

  4. Let Vosa learn your voice

    Talk for twenty seconds. Two beeps tell you it is done.

Five languages

Say it the way you already say it.

Vosa works out what you mean, then reads it back before any money moves.

Amounts work the way you count them: 5k, 2k5, two thousand five, dubu biyar, puku ise.

Voice Promise

The person who stops you is you.

When you start a goal, you record why it matters. Try to take the money out early and Vosa plays your own voice back, then asks one question: is this an emergency?

It is the witness at ajo, without the witness. Your money is never locked. You decide in advance what an emergency needs.

Promise Score

Keeping your word becomes your record.

A lender wants to know one thing: will you keep a commitment when money is tight? Vosa sees that moment, and keeps count.

Raises your score

  • Leaving a goal alone when tempted
  • Reaching a goal
  • Repaying on time

Lowers it

  • Taking money out early
  • Repaying late

Need the money anyway? Borrow against your goal.

Borrow up to half of what you have saved there. The goal stays whole, and you repay by voice.

In this preview the score and loans use demo money. It is not a credit report, and no lender is involved yet.

Questions

Plain answers.

What Vosa is today, and what it is not yet.

Is Vosa a bank?

No. Vosa is the way you talk to your money. It is designed to work with licensed savings partners, who hold the money and pay the interest. In this early preview, all balances are demo money.

Can a blind person really sign up alone?

After one tap, yes: everything is asked and answered out loud. Opening Vosa the first time may still need a screen reader or a helper. If someone helps, Vosa asks them to step away before you say your secret phrase.

Does my voice leave my phone?

Your voiceprint, the pattern Voice ID uses to recognise you, is made and kept on your phone. What you say is sent to our speech partner, Spitch, only to be turned into words.

What if someone copies my voice?

Voice ID is one check of three. A copy of your voice still needs your secret phrase and three numbers that change every time. For big transfers you can add your phone's fingerprint or face, or have a helper approve.

What if I can't speak clearly?

Type instead, use the large buttons, or use your phone's screen reader. Everything Vosa does by voice can also be done on the screen.

Which languages does Vosa speak?

English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yorùbá, Hausa and Igbo. Vosa switches when you switch. Hausa and Igbo are the newest and are still being checked by native speakers.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open vosa.vercel.app on your phone. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
