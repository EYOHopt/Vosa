What Vosa is today, and what it will become.

Is Vosa a bank?

No. Vosa is the way you talk to your money. It is designed to work with licensed savings partners, who hold the money and pay the interest. In this early preview, balances are simulated.

Does my voice leave my phone?

Your voiceprint, the pattern Voice ID uses to recognise you, is made and kept on your phone and never leaves it. What you say is sent to our speech partner, Spitch, only to be turned into words, and is not kept.

What if someone copies my voice?

Voice ID is one check out of several. You also need your secret phrase and three numbers that change every time. For big transfers you can add your phone's fingerprint or face, or ask a trusted helper to approve.

What if I can't speak clearly?

You can type instead, use large buttons, or use your phone's screen reader. Vosa is voice-first, never voice-only.

Which languages does Vosa speak?

English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yorùbá, Hausa and Igbo. Vosa switches automatically when you switch. Hausa and Igbo are newly added and being reviewed by native speakers.

How do I sign in on a new phone?

Sign in with your phone number and your secret phrase. Your savings, goals and helpers come back. Voice ID is made again on the new phone, because your voiceprint never leaves the old one.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open vosa.vercel.app on your phone. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
